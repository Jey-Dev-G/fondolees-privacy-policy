Privacy Policy for FondoLess

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

FondoLess is a mobile application developed by Jey Dev. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in the FondoLess mobile application for Android.

Information Collection and Use

FondoLess is built as a 100% local (On-Device) application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user information, or media files with third parties or external servers.

Image Processing

All photo editing, background removal, and masking processes are executed entirely on your device using local frameworks (OpenCV and Google ML Kit). Temporary images are saved strictly in the application's secure cache directory (cacheDir) and are destroyed when no longer needed.

Permissions

FondoLess uses the system's native Android Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia), which allows you to select photos without granting broad storage permissions to the application.

Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since FondoLess operates entirely offline and does not collect any data, no children's data is ever captured, stored, or transmitted.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
